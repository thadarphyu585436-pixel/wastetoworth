Keep the Environment Clean

Everyone has a responsibility to help keep the environment clean and safe. People should avoid littering in public places, parks, streets, and waterways because waste pollution can harm animals, damage ecosystems, and create unhealthy surroundings. Participating in community clean-up activities and encouraging others to recycle are good ways to protect the environment. Simple actions such as throwing rubbish into the correct bin and reducing waste can make a big difference. By working together, communities can create a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable future for everyone.
