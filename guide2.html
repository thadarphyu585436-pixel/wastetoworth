Clean Recyclable Materials

Recyclable materials should be cleaned before being placed into recycling bins. Bottles, cans, jars, and containers should be rinsed to remove leftover food, liquids, or dirt. Wet or dirty recyclable items can contaminate other materials and affect the recycling process. For example, oily paper or food-covered containers may not be accepted by recycling facilities. Cleaning recyclable items helps maintain hygiene, improves recycling efficiency, and increases the chance that materials can be reused to create new products.
