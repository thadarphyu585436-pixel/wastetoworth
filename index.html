Reduce, Reuse, Recycle for a Better Planet


Let's Sort, Recycle & Protect Our Planet

Recycling different types of waste is an important part of waste management. It helps reduce pollution, save energy, and conserve natural resources. Materials like plastic, paper, glass, metal, and organic waste can be separated and recycled in different ways to be reused. By recycling, we can reduce landfill waste and protect the environment for the future.

Purpose of this website

The purpose of this website is to raise awareness about recycling and teach people how to manage different types of waste such as plastic, paper, glass, and organic materials. It provides simple and useful information on how to reduce waste, reuse materials, and recycle properly to help protect the environment. The website also highlights the negative impacts of improper waste disposal, including pollution and harm to wildlife and natural resources. Based on our Essential Question — How can we design an awareness website about recycling different types of waste materials in the local community to reduce waste, save natural resources, and protect the environment? — it aims to encourage the local community to take responsible actions and develop sustainable habits for a cleaner and greener future.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is recycling important?

It helps reduce pollution, save natural resources, and protect the environment

What happens if we don't recycle?

Waste builds up, causing pollution, environmental damage, and health problems.

How does recycling help the environment?

Recycling reduces the amount of waste sent to landfills, saves energy, and lowers pollution. It also helps protect wildlife and natural habitats.

What happens if I mix recyclable and non-recyclable waste?

If waste is mixed, it can contaminate recyclable materials, making them harder or impossible to recycle.
