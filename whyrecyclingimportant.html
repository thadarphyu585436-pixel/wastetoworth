Simple Ways to Help

Separate Waste At Home

Sort your waste into different categories such as plastic, paper, glass, and food waste. This makes recycling easier and helps reduce the amount of trash sent to landfills.

Reuse Bags and Bottles

Use reusable shopping bags and water bottles instead of throwing away single-use items. Reusing products helps reduce plastic waste and protects the environment.

Use Recycling Bins

Place recyclable materials in the correct recycling bins. Proper recycling helps save energy, conserve natural resources, and reduce pollution.
