Dispose of Hazardous Waste Safely

Hazardous waste should always be disposed of carefully because it contains harmful substances that can damage the environment and human health. Batteries, chemicals, paints, electronic devices, and fluorescent light bulbs should never be thrown into normal household bins. These materials may contain toxic chemicals that can pollute soil and water if disposed of improperly. Hazardous waste should be taken to special recycling or collection centers where it can be handled safely. Proper disposal helps prevent environmental pollution and protects the health of communities.
