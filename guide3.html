Reduce Single-Use Plastics

People should reduce the use of single-use plastics because they create large amounts of waste and pollution. Plastic bags, straws, cups, and disposable utensils are often used only once before being thrown away. These materials can take many years to decompose and may harm animals and the environment. Instead of using disposable products, people should choose reusable alternatives such as cloth shopping bags, reusable water bottles, and metal straws. Reducing plastic use helps decrease pollution, conserve natural resources, and protect oceans and wildlife.
